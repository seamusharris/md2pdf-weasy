## Overview

The **wnc theme** captures Warrandyte Netball Club's design system. A single
bold red (`#DC0032`) anchors everything — buttons, table headers, accent
underlines — with quiet navy links and a sans-serif type stack so content
carries first.

- **Body type:** Open Sans
- **Headings:** Montserrat semibold (quiet — same colour as body)
- **Brand:** `#DC0032` red
- **Links:** `#14376b` navy (quiet by default, red on hover in web contexts)

## Typography

Body text is Open Sans. Headings are Montserrat semibold — and **the same
colour as body text**. The hierarchy comes from size and weight, not from
shouting in brand red. The brand-red underline accent under section
headings (h2) is the signature WNC mark.

### Section headings

Note the brand-red underline beneath the h2 above — that's the WNC mark.
It scales with the text and works across light and dark backgrounds.

#### Smaller headings

The same Montserrat semibold treatment cascades to h3 and h4, just at
smaller sizes. Body text fills paragraphs at 16px default — comfortable
on screen and in print.


## Callouts

> **Note:** Same callout palette as the base — info navy border, light
> navy background. WNC theme inherits the bundled callout colours.

> **Tip:** Teal callout for tips and suggestions.

> **Important:** Purple callout for important information.

> **Caution:** Amber callout for caution notices.

> **Warning:** Red callout — uses the bundled warn red (`#d94040`), which
> the wnc theme keeps rather than overriding to brand red, so warnings
> stay distinct from brand accents.
